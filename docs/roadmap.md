# Roadmap

Slices 1–31 are done. Each milestone below gets its own spec and turns into slices
numbered from 32 on, added here as unticked boxes. Tick a slice in the commit that
delivers it.

## Milestones: elite houses, first level

Every elite keeps the population and consumption of the four Townhouses it replaces, adds
one consumption of its own, and comes with an annex across a road. The annex is staffed
from the labor pool and is where the bonus is made.

- [ ] **Elite rework.** The Traders' Guild keeps its 240 people, the merchants become the
  yard's workers, it pays low taxes, and its charter becomes Well dressed (a Townhouse
  exists and every Townhouse has fleece and oil, 24 months). Guild sites become elite
  sites, for every type.
- [ ] **Scholars' House.** Near a Theater, a Gymnasium and a Podium. Charter: Learned city
  (full culture coverage, 24 months). Consumes scrolls, from papyrus imports or fleece via
  a Scriptorium. Annex: Library. Bonus: Works of Art, at most one a year, spent on an
  upgrade tree.
- [ ] **Warriors' House.** Near a Hospital. Charter: Healthy and well fed (24 months),
  which brings sickness. Consumes armor (bronze → Armory). Annex: Drill Ground. Bonus:
  hoplites on the army roster.
- [ ] **Athletes' House.** Near a Stadium. Charter: full Gymnasium coverage with a Stadium
  built (24 months). Consumes double oil. Annex: Bathhouse, which raises appeal. Bonus:
  the city enters the Olympics every 4 years, with its chance of winning in the Ledger.
- [ ] **Merchant Mariners.** Near the shore. Charter: Watching the Waves (fishermen at sea,
  24 months), which brings fishing. Consumes wood and cloth to build the ship, then for
  repairs. Low taxes. Annex: Dock. Bonus: a sea route.
- [ ] **Trireme Crew.** Near the shore. Charter: the Warriors' one, and its permit fits
  either house. Consumes wood and armor. Annex: Dock. Bonus: a warship on the roster.

Later: elite levels (Horsemen), Nobles' Court near the Palace.
